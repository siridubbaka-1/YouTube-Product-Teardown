The Attention Engine

The right content needs to reach the right person at the right time. Recommendation is the heart of this engine.

Why cracking attention matters

STEP 01
If users see relevant and interesting content, they watch, scroll and spend more time.
›
STEP 02
Once users come to relevant videos, relevant ads also can reach the right audience.
›
STEP 03
Advertisers happy, generates more revenue. Platform and creators happy, creates more. Users watch more.

Among thousands of relevant videos — what gets recommended first?

YouTube looks at these six signals to decide priority.

01 Click-Through Rate (CTR)

Out of everyone who saw the thumbnail, how many actually clicked? High CTR means the title and thumbnail created curiosity.

I ate world's largest Pizza
8:03
10%
10 clicks out of 100 times shown
02 Average Watch Time

Average time a viewer spends on a video. A video with fewer views but high watch time beats a viral video nobody finishes.

03 Retention

How many viewers are still watching as the video goes on? It tells YouTube exactly where people lose interest and which videos hold people till the end.

05 Session Time

The total time between when a user opens YouTube and when they close it. YouTube optimises for this above individual video metrics — a video that ends a session is penalised even if it is long.
